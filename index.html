About the product

Meet Kat Loco

KAT loco is a foot-based VR locomotion system that gives you natural control over walking, running, and turning inside virtual reality.

Founded in 2013, KAT VR focuses on research, development, and delivery of locomotion hardware that helps immersive content feel more physical, intuitive, and believable.

The system combines ergonomic wearability, wireless tracking, and flexible software controls so players and professionals can move with confidence.

Frequently asked questions

Clear Answers

Common questions about comfort, compatibility, and the overall locomotion experience.

What makes KAT loco different from other locomotion systems? +

KAT loco combines wireless lower-body tracking, smooth movement mapping, and compact physical requirements so users can move more naturally without needing a large dedicated room.

Will it help reduce motion sickness? +

Matching foot movement with what users see in the headset can improve comfort for many people, especially when compared with abrupt movement methods like instant teleport transitions.

Which VR headsets is it compatible with? +

KatVR is designed for broad compatibility across popular PC VR ecosystems and experiences that support locomotion-based input.

Can I use it for training or design reviews, not only games? +

Yes. The same movement system is useful for education, simulations, fitness, architecture walk-throughs, and social VR scenarios that benefit from active navigation.
